About AyurHerb

A Data-Driven Ayurvedic Wellness Recommendation System bridging classical herbal formulations with modern machine learning text analytics.

Educational Scope & Mandatory Medical Disclaimer

The application is intended for educational and informational purposes and should not be used as a substitute for professional medical advice, diagnosis, or clinical prescription. AyurHerb does not claim to diagnose diseases or guarantee medical cures. All recommendations reflect historical and traditional wellness literature cataloged in the AyurGenixAI dataset.

1. Project Objective & Problem Statement

Ayurveda is one of the world's oldest holistic healing systems, emphasizing balance among mind, body, and the three biological humors (Doshas: Vata, Pitta, and Kapha). However, navigating traditional Ayurvedic dravyas (herbs), formulations, pathya (dietary rules), and lifestyle guidance based on symptoms is complex.

AyurHerb addresses this challenge by applying Natural Language Processing (NLP) and Information Retrieval techniques on a curated clinical dataset (AyurGenixAI), allowing users to input everyday symptom descriptions and discover historically relevant Ayurvedic herbs, home remedies, and lifestyle routines.

2. The AyurGenixAI Dataset

  • Seasonal Variation (Ritucharya)
  • Medical Interventions & Precautions

3. NLP Recommendation Algorithm

The recommendation engine uses a fully self-contained machine learning pipeline implemented via Scikit-learn without reliance on external commercial AI APIs:

A. Text Preprocessing & Cleaning (preprocess.py)

Raw text undergoes lowercasing, punctuation stripping, whitespace normalization, and composite feature synthesis. Missing values are filled with domain-appropriate fallbacks.

B. TF-IDF Vectorization (Term Frequency - Inverse Document Frequency)

TF-IDF converts text documents and user symptom queries into high-dimensional numerical vectors, penalizing overly frequent generic words while rewarding clinically distinguishing symptom terms.

\(\text{TF-IDF}(t, d, D) = \text{TF}(t, d) \times \log\left(\frac{1 + |D|}{1 + \text{DF}(t, D)}\right) + 1\)
C. Cosine Similarity Scoring & Thresholding

Measures the cosine of the angle between the user query vector \(\vec{u}\) and dataset symptom vector \(\vec{v}\):

\(\text{Cosine Similarity}(\vec{u}, \vec{v}) = \frac{\vec{u} \cdot \vec{v}}{\|\vec{u}\| \|\vec{v}\|} = \frac{\sum_{i=1}^n u_i v_i}{\sqrt{\sum_{i=1}^n u_i^2} \sqrt{\sum_{i=1}^n v_i^2}}\)

Matches above the configurable threshold (≥ 12-15%) are ranked and returned with transparent Symptom Similarity Scores.

4. Clinical Safety & Emergency Triage (safety.py)

To prevent misuse during medical emergencies, AyurHerb runs a high-priority safety evaluation on all user queries prior to vector matching. If symptoms indicating severe difficulty breathing, crushing chest pain, convulsions, or severe hemorrhage are detected, standard herbal recommendations are withheld and the user is redirected to emergency clinical services.
