Data Science & EDA Methodology

The visualizations above are programmatically computed using Pandas, NumPy, Matplotlib, and Seaborn. Textual fields such as Ayurvedic Herbs and Formulation contain composite multi-ingredient strings which were tokenized, cleaned of punctuation, and normalized into frequency distributions.

Data Cleaning & Standardization:

Imputed missing values, unified casing, and created a 35th unified search vector column.

Cross-Tabulation & Heatmaps:

Mapped disease categories against seasonal variables and symptom severities.
