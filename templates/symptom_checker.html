How to Select Your Severity Level

Severity helps our Ayurvedic engine determine whether to suggest gentle restorative teas, standard decoctions, or more concentrated herbal formulations:

🟢 Mild Slight / Early Stage

You notice minor discomfort (e.g. occasional cough, slight throat tickle, mild fatigue), but you can easily work, study, eat, and sleep without interruption.

🟡 Moderate (Default) Noticeable & Uncomfortable

Symptoms are clearly noticeable (e.g. persistent cough, digestive discomfort, body ache). It slows you down, affects focus or sleep, and requires rest or home care.

🔴 Significant / Severe Intense & Disruptive

Symptoms are severe and prevent you from performing daily tasks or sleeping peacefully.
